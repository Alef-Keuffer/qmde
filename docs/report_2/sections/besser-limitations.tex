\begin{itemize}[nosep]

	      optima differ.

	\item QAOA only, on a simulator, at most 18 qubits; the Baseline enumerates every schedule.
	\item $\lambda = (\Delta+1)w$ is derived from \cite[§2.3]{Lucas}; the scale $s$ of the angles has no
	      reference.
	\item Seven workarounds for the course fork (\texttt{besser/transformations/model\_io.py}, W1--W7).
	\item The instances are not yet those of the EMF chain (names, weights, slots of the path).
\end{itemize}}
